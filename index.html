Season highlights

Arianna Lau — T19 at the Augusta National Women's Amateur

68-71-75 (−2), Apr 1–4. First golfer from Hong Kong, China ever to make the ANWA 36-hole cut, then closed at Augusta National. Also reached the last 16 of the 123rd Women's Amateur Championship at Muirfield (Jun).

Queen Sirikit Cup — team bronze

Hong Kong, China finished 3rd of 13 (557) behind Japan and Korea at Sentul Highlands, Indonesia (May 12–15). Team: Arianna Lau (279), Sabrina Wong (284), Felicia Hughes — Hughes on international debut.

Tian Jun — five wins and counting (WAGR #175)

Won 4 WAGR events in China (JLJ Points Championship, Feng Shanshan Chengdu, CWJPGA Zhuhai, World Junior Elite Chengdu) plus the HK Junior Close overall title with a second-round 63. T3 at FCG International (67-69-71, −9) in San Diego.

Peiqi (Peggy) Hou — FCG International champion

Won Girls 13-14 at the 19th FCG International, Pala Mesa, California (Jun 29–Jul 3) at −12 (65-67=132), with teammate Doris Sung T2 (−8). Also won SLGS FCG China Series Shanghai in March.

Sabrina Wong — two wins on the European circuit

Won the Triple A European Invitational (Jan) and the Triple A World Final U21 Girls (Mar) in Spain — the only player in the entire field under par. 3rd Scottish Girls' Open, 5th R&A Girls U16 (as defending champion), 5th Welsh Open SP, 5th German International Girls, T7 APGC Junior.

Felicia Hughes — Singapore Junior champion

Won Girls Division A and the Girls Overall title at the Singapore Junior Golf Championship, Keppel Club (Jun 13), by 2 shots. Queen Sirikit Cup debut in May; T74 at Junior World G15-18 in July.

Siuue Wu — NCAA record-tier season + US Women's Open

Florida sophomore: career-best T2 at the Gators Invitational (65-69-71), T14 at the NCAA Championship where her second-round 64 (−8) tied the championship single-round record vs par, All-Tournament Team, All-SEC 2nd team. Qualified for and played the 81st US Women's Open at Riviera (MC).

Junior World Championships — 28-player HK squad

Best HK finishes at the USWING Mojing Junior World (San Diego, Jul 7–9): Stella Yang 7th (G7-8), Doris Sung T12 (G13-14, closing 67), Peggy Hou T20, Joseph Cao & Thorsten Iu T22 (B13-14), Albert Sun T22 (B9-10), Vivianne Kan T29 (G15-18).

Joseph Cao — four wins at 13

Won the CWJPGA Carnival National Grand Finals, Guangdong Junior Tour #2, FCG Callaway World Championship Chengdu, and the HK Junior Close Boys 13-14 title; 2nd at FCG International B13-14 (−7); 15th at the APGC Junior Championship.

Jeffrey Shen — HK's No.1 male amateur on the road

Represented Hong Kong, China at the 131st Amateur Championship, Royal Liverpool (Jun 15–20; 288-player field). 2026 campaign spans Spain, Scotland, England, with the HK Open, AAC and Q-School ahead.

Alanna Tee — HK Ladies Close champion at 14

Won the Hong Kong Ladies Close Amateur (Jan 28–30, HKGC), then 3rd Girls Overall at the HK Junior Close, plus starts in the Philippines, Thailand, Malaysia and Singapore.

Isaac Timso — international runner-up

2nd in the Development Division at the Mandiri Ciputra Golfpreneur Junior World Championship, Jakarta (Jun 10–12, 223) and co-champion of the HK Junior Close Boys 11-12.
